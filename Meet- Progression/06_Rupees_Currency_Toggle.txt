WHAT WAS THE GOAL?
------------------
To support Indian enterprise and retail stakeholders by providing an instant, interactive currency switcher on the dashboard. When managers review marketing budgets and sales uplifts, they should be able to view and export all financial metrics in Indian Rupees (INR) alongside US Dollars (USD).


WHAT I DID AS MEET:
-------------------

1. Added the Currency Switcher Widget (`src/dashboard/app.py`):
   - Implemented a radio toggle directly at the top of the sidebar control panel:
     * Option 1: Rupees (INR) [Selected by default]
     * Option 2: US Dollars (USD)
   - Clean, professional styling with zero emoji characters to preserve the corporate aesthetic.

2. Dynamic Financial Formatting Across All Components:
   - When Rupees is selected, the currency symbol dynamically updates to INR across every section:
     * Budget Slider: Dynamically labels "Marketing / Promotion Budget (INR)".
     * Top Executive KPI Cards: Displays Allocated Capital, Incremental Revenue Lift, and Avoided Inefficient Spend in INR.
     * Confounding Breakdown Bar Chart: Formats the true causal lift, organic foot traffic bias, and Y-axis labels in INR.
     * Strategy Benchmark Chart: Shows comparative revenue across Knapsack, High-Spender, and Random allocation in INR.
     * Qini Uplift Curve: Scales cumulative incremental return in INR.
     * Candidate Allocation Table: Displays Historical Sales (INR) and Predicted Uplift (INR).

3. Localized Export Capabilities:
   - The downloadable action list dynamically tags column headers with the active currency and names the file accordingly: `EconoCausal_Allocation_Schedule_INR.csv`.
   - Operations teams can immediately hand this file to local marketing teams with no manual currency conversions needed.

4. Quality and Unit Testing:
   - Re-verified zero-emoji compliance across the codebase.
   - Tested full test suite: all 8 automated tests passed in 4.16 seconds.


KEY RESULT & TAKEAWAY:
----------------------
The platform now natively supports Indian enterprise deployments! Store managers and corporate directors can toggle between Indian Rupees and US Dollars in one click, seeing all budget constraints, incremental revenue uplifts, and operational schedules formatted accordingly.
