WHAT IS THIS FOLDER?
--------------------
Hey there! This folder is my personal, transparent progress log for the EconoCausal project.
Before every major code commit or milestone, I write an entry here in plain, everyday human language.

The goal is simple:
1. Anyone (my team members, mentors, or evaluators) can open these files and immediately understand what I did, why I did it, and what problem it solves.
2. No heavy academic jargon without a simple explanation.
3. Keep an honest record of experiments, challenges faced, decisions made, and technical victories.
